**An open protocol for accounting data in Europe** – one harmonised model for bookings, receipts, invoices, reports
and filings, with the existing European formats as translations.
<sub>a [falkOSS](https://falkoss.org) project</sub>

> librotenado is at its beginning: nothing is published yet. Start with the
> [whitepaper](https://github.com/librotenado/whitepaper).

- **One model** – designed once and well, instead of one format per country.
- **Compatible with what exists** – EN 16931 and Peppol, national e-invoicing systems, SAF-T, ISO 20022, XBRL and
  national charts of accounts translate into and out of it.
- **Made for synchronisation** – an event model with end-to-end encryption, for apps, relays and servers of any vendor.
- **Open for everyone** – specification under CC BY 4.0, reference library under MIT.

The first implementation is [spezado](https://github.com/spezado), free accounting for everyone.
